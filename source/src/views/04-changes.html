Why your timeline will be your own

Bodies differ

Change varies widely

How much breast growth, facial hair or voice change you get varies widely between people, much as it did in puberty. In one study, blood estradiol levels didn't predict how much breast growth happened in the first year.

Patience

Slow is normal

Breast growth is mostly in the first six months, then volume keeps creeping up for at least three years. Facial hair keeps filling in for four to five years. In studies, blood hormone levels didn't predict how much breast growth or voice change people had, and higher doses of cyproterone brought more side effects without lowering testosterone further.

Comparison

Online timelines aren't yours

Photo timelines online are usually the most dramatic cases. Comparing with your own photos from a few months ago is kinder and more accurate.

What stays

Bones don't change shape

Once growth plates have closed in adulthood, hormones don't change height, the size of hands and feet, the width of the shoulders and hips, or the shape of the jaw. Surgery, voice training, hair removal and clothing can do things hormones can't.
